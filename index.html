About

I am a researcher in genetics, genomics, and plant science with a focus on climate-resilient crops and sustainable agricultural production. My work combines biotechnology and computational genomics with multi-omics and systems biology approaches to identify candidate genes involved in abiotic and biotic stress tolerance.

Areas of expertise

  • Genetics and Plant Breeding
  • Genomics
  • Bioinformatics
  • System Biology
  • Plant Molecular Biology

Research focus

My background in biotechnology and computational genomics drives my interest in integrating conventional techniques with genomic methodologies. I focus on crafting climate-resilient crops for sustainable agricultural production and on using large genomic datasets to improve resilience against environmental challenges.
