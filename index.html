Welcome

Welcome to the research page of the Center for Crop Genomics and Systems Biology. Our research focuses on genomics, systems biology, and AI-assisted breeding approaches for cereals, legumes, and climate-resilient crop improvement.

Research

Our research integrates genomics, bioinformatics, systems biology, and artificial intelligence to understand complex traits in cereals and legumes. We focus on climate resilience, stress tolerance, genomic prediction, and next-generation breeding strategies to accelerate sustainable crop improvement under changing environmental conditions.

By combining multi-omics datasets with computational and experimental approaches, we aim to identify key molecular mechanisms underlying yield stability, adaptation, and resilience in major crop species.
